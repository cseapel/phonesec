Listing Copy

PhoneSec — Privacy Watchdog
Short description (80 char max)

See which apps use your mic, camera and location — the moment they do.

Long description (4000 char max)

PhoneSec watches your phone's input devices and tells you, in real time, which apps are using them. Microphone, camera, GPS, keyboard, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth — all monitored, all visible.

Hero feature — a floating bubble appears the moment any app silently accesses a sensor in the background. Tap it to inspect the app, see its full access history, and block it.

Privacy-first by design — everything happens on your device. No accounts, no telemetry, no cloud. Your activity log auto-prunes after 30 days.

What you get:
• Live dashboard with privacy-index score
• Real-time activity feed across all apps
• Per-app 24-hour heatmap and timeline
• Traffic-light risk rating
• 7-day threat history with grouped incidents
• Configurable alert delivery — bubble, banner, fullscreen or status bar

Export Notes

To export at Play's required resolutions, take screenshots of this page (or the prototype) at the listed sizes: icon 512×512, feature graphic 1024×500, phone screenshots 1080×1920 each. Browser DevTools device emulation or a Puppeteer script will produce pixel-exact PNGs. The visuals here are scaled for layout — boost the base font + element sizes proportionally when capturing for upload.
