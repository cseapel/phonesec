● features

Six sensors. Every app. All the time.

PhoneSec watches your phone's input devices and surfaces the moment any app touches them — even from the background.

Real-time monitoring

Mic, camera, location, keyboard, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth — tracked continuously across every installed app.

Caught-in-the-act bubble

A floating chat-head appears the instant an app silently uses a sensor in the background. Tap to inspect.

24-hour heatmap

Tap any app to see a per-sensor heatmap and a full timeline of foreground vs background access.

Traffic-light risk

Every app gets a clear safe / warn / danger rating computed from its background access patterns.

7-day threat history

Grouped incident log shows everything caught in the last week — perfect for pattern-spotting.

Configurable alerts

Bubble, banner, fullscreen takeover, or status-bar dot. Pick the sensitivity and per-sensor channels you want.
