● Privacy Policy

PhoneSec

Effective May 4, 2026 · Version 1.0
The short version

PhoneSec runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no advertising.

1. Who we are

PhoneSec ("we", "us") is the developer of the PhoneSec Android application (the "App"). You can reach us at [YOUR_EMAIL]. Mailing address: [YOUR_ADDRESS].

2. What the App does

The App monitors which other applications installed on your Android device access your phone's input devices — microphone, camera, location (GPS), keyboard, Wi-Fi, and Bluetooth — and shows you a real-time dashboard, an activity history, and an alert when an app accesses an input device while running in the background.

3. Information we access on your device

To do its job, the App reads the following information locally on your device only:

DataWhyWhere it goes
Installed appsTo list apps and show their permissionsStays on device
Permission usage events (mic, cam, location, etc.)To build the activity feed and risk scoreStays on device
App foreground / background stateTo detect background accessStays on device
Wi-Fi / Bluetooth scan eventsTo attribute scans to appsStays on device
User preferences (alert style, density, theme)To remember your settingsStays on device

4. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not read the contents of your microphone, camera, files, messages, contacts, photos, or keystrokes.
  • We do not create user accounts.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics, crash reporters, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not transmit any data off your device to any server we control or any third party.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information.

5. Permissions we request

PermissionPurpose
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATSSee which apps used the microphone, camera, location, etc.
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOWShow the floating alert bubble over other apps
POST_NOTIFICATIONSDeliver alerts when an app is caught accessing a sensor
FOREGROUND_SERVICEKeep monitoring active in the background
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGESEnumerate installed apps to monitor them
ACCESS_WIFI_STATE / BLUETOOTH_CONNECTDetect Wi-Fi and Bluetooth scans by other apps

You can revoke any permission from Settings → Apps → PhoneSec → Permissions at any time. The App will continue to function for any feature whose permission has not been revoked.

6. Data storage and retention

All data is stored in a local database on your device. Activity events are automatically deleted after 30 days. Uninstalling the App removes everything immediately. There is no backup of phonesec data to Google or to us.

7. Your rights

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You control everything by interacting with the App or uninstalling it. Under GDPR (EEA/UK), CCPA (California) and similar regulations, you nonetheless have the right to contact us about your privacy at [YOUR_EMAIL].

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from children — and as noted, we do not collect data from anyone.

9. Security

The local database is private to the App's sandbox per Android's standard app isolation. We recommend keeping your device locked, your OS updated, and Play Protect enabled.

10. Third parties

The App contains no third-party SDKs, trackers, advertising libraries, or analytics. The only external party involved in distribution is Google Play, governed by Google's own privacy policy.

11. Changes to this policy

If we materially change this policy, we will update the "Effective" date above and surface the change in-app before it takes effect. Past versions are available on request.

12. Contact
